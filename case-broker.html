Brief

The problem

Modelled on the big listing portals, for a client who will have no tech team after handover. The scope is a main website, a landing page and 500 listings loaded before handover, then unlimited listings added by the client through the admin.

That shaped every decision: owners can post their own listings, staff can import a spreadsheet, and security lives in the database so it can't be skipped by mistake.

What's inside

What I built

01Public search

Home with search, categories, latest and popular localities; a search page with filters, sort and a price-pin map.

02Property pages

Gallery, facts, map, schema.org markup, and call-back and site-visit requests verified by an inline OTP.

03City and locality pages

Pages such as /in/mumbai/andheri-east for local search traffic, plus a sitemap and robots file.

04Owners post themselves

A four-step form with photos and review. New listings always land in 'Awaiting review'; editing a live one sends it back for review.

05Admin panel

Listing form with a map pin and photos, a review queue, a leads inbox with CSV export, and staff roles.

06Bulk import

Upload a CSV or Excel sheet. 'Check file' validates every row and lists problems by row and column; 'Import' saves the valid rows. Re-importing a fixed file updates listings instead of duplicating them. Prices accept 85000, 45 L or 1.2 Cr.

07Mobile-OTP login

Phone number and a one-time code, with a 5-minute expiry, a 60-second resend gap, rate limits and a bot check.

08Fast pages

Public pages are cached and refreshed the moment an admin or owner changes a listing.

Design

Design decisions

A street-sign brandMumbai road-sign blue, taxi yellow and black-and-yellow chequer stripes under the header and around the type rail. Localities appear as street-sign plates.
TypeGloock for headlines and Schibsted Grotesk for UI and prices, with tabular figures so numbers line up.
Honest imageryThe hero is a generated tower at blue hour, used as atmosphere only. Listings without photos show no placeholder art, so nothing pretends to be a real property.
Screens

The build

On a phone the layout is rebuilt for thumbs, not just squeezed: larger targets, a simpler header and the same content in a single column.
