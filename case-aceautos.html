Brief

The problem

Window film is bought per car, not per roll. A customer needs the price for their model, in the range they want, with or without a sunroof.

So the catalogue is organised by make, and each product carries the price matrix for every model of that make.

What's inside

What I built

01V-KOOL heat control film, by make

A product per manufacturer (Mercedes-Benz, Audi, BMW, Jeep, Volkswagen, MG, Mahindra, Honda, Toyota, Kia, Maruti Suzuki, Skoda, Tata, Hyundai and more). Pick your model, range and sunroof option; the price shown covers the full car.

02SunFlect nano-ceramic film

Individual shades such as IR Blue 80, IR Green 70, HD Blue 70 and HD Charcoal 70, priced by body type (hatchback, sedan, SUV) with and without a sunroof, each with its own SKU.

03Signature series, enquire only

Higher-end film lines list heat-rejection percentages per variant and are tagged 'Enquire only' so they lead to a conversation, not a checkout.

04Lighting

LED driving and fog lights sold in sets, with the lens and beam details in the description.

05Stock-aware variants

Inventory is tracked per variant, so only combinations the shop can fit show as available.
