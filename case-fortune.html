Brief

The problem

A detailing studio sells 34+ services across polish, ceramic, PPF, wraps and tint, and every job takes a different number of days. The goal was to quote the right price for the exact car, in two minutes, without double-booking a bay.

So the calendar has real capacity rules, and the studio gets a panel to manage bookings, holidays and walk-ins.

What's inside

What I built

01Instant pricing

Pick your car and body type and every price updates to fit.

02Six service families

Wash & Care, Interior, Paint & Polish, Protection, Wraps & Styling, and Tint & Glass.

03Annual care plans

Essential, Plus (most popular) and Elite. Pay once and use credits within 12 months.

04Live calendar

Customers see which days are open. A new web request is 'Pending' and holds its dates for 48 hours until the studio confirms on WhatsApp.

05WhatsApp confirmation

The request is saved to the studio's database and opens as a pre-filled WhatsApp message.

06Studio panel (/admin)

See every booking with search and filters; call or WhatsApp the customer; confirm, start, finish, cancel or mark a no-show; a month view of cars per day; close days for holidays; change capacity, opening days and hold time; add walk-ins.

07Capacity rules

Three cars at a time. Multi-day jobs (ceramic, PPF, wraps, polish) hold a bay on every working day they run, at most two at once so a bay stays free for quick jobs. The server re-checks on every booking.

08Brands strip

A scrolling row of the brands the studio works with, ready for real logos.

Design

Design decisions

Dark, red and directA near-black canvas with one red accent for actions, so the booking buttons are always the loudest thing on screen.
Plain stepsYour car, choose your services, pick a time, booking received. Four short steps with the estimate always visible.
Screens

The build

On a phone the layout is rebuilt for thumbs, not just squeezed: larger targets, a simpler header and the same content in a single column.

Stack

Built with

HTML, CSS and JavaScriptVercel serverless functionsVercel Blob (bookings storage)WhatsApp deep linksOptional Razorpay advance (built, not enabled)
SecurityCredentials live only in environment variables. Sessions are signed, HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict cookies lasting 12 hours; 8 wrong passwords in 15 minutes lock that device out; the admin is no-index, uncached and has a strict Content-Security-Policy; customer text is always escaped.
Abuse limitsThe public booking endpoint ignores payment claims, has a honeypot field and allows 3 live requests per phone number.
