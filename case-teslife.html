Brief

The problem

Most wrap shops sell with a gallery and a phone number. Teslife needed something closer to configuring a car online: see the finished colour first, see the price, then book a drop-off without back-and-forth.

The site is built like an app: a configurator home, searchable services and parts, a three-step booking flow and a way to track the job afterwards.

What's inside

What I built

01Configurator home

Pick your Tesla, tap a wrap colour and the car repaints. Gloss, satin and matte read differently. Choose Avery or 3M film, then add parts, tint or chrome delete; lips, spoilers, mirror caps, tint and chrome delete show on the car.

02Look packs

One tap adds a gloss black or carbon look set. Plain groupings, no implied discount.

03Services and parts

Searchable lists that filter to your model, so nothing irrelevant shows up.

04Three-step booking

Car and items, then start day and drop-off time, then details. The calendar knows how long the job keeps the car (wraps take about four working days) and shows the span. Full days are struck through, with the next free days suggested.

05Done screen and hand-off

A booking reference, what happens next, and pre-filled Text and WhatsApp messages. If the booking can't be saved (offline, 10-second timeout) the details go by text instead of being lost. The screen survives a refresh for 48 hours.

06Track a job

Look up a booking by reference or plate plus phone number.

07Real 3D, only when needed

Rotatable 3D models for Model 3, S, Y, X and Cybertruck. three.js loads only when a 3D car is on screen, and a side-view drawing takes over when WebGL is off.

08Light, dark and phone-first

Follows the system theme. Phones get a bottom tab bar.

Design

Design decisions

Apple 'Buy' page styleA big product stage with the options beside it. The car is the interface.
Honest pricingPrices are shown as published; items with no published price book as a quote instead of guessing.
Capacity rules in the openThe calendar shows exactly what the shop can take, so customers pick days that will actually work.
Screens

The build

On a phone the layout is rebuilt for thumbs, not just squeezed: larger targets, a simpler header and the same content in a single column.
