Overview

The challenge

Maulika Sharma is a fitness coach who needed a website that would do two jobs simultaneously — act as a professional portfolio and serve as an active client onboarding tool.

The core challenge was designing for a mobile-first audience of prospective coaching clients who discover coaches on Instagram and need to be converted into paying clients within a single website visit.

🎯
The Problem
No website, no way to convert Instagram followers into coaching clients. Discovery to booking involved too many steps.
✅
The Goal
Design a warm, energetic website that builds trust immediately and makes booking a session feel simple and natural.
Process

How I approached it

1
User Journey Mapping
Mapped the typical journey of a prospective coaching client — from Instagram discovery to website visit to booking — and identified every friction point along the way.
2
Content Architecture
Structured the site around three core user goals: understand who Maulika is, understand what she offers, and book a session. Each section answers one question.
3
Visual Identity
Created a warm, energetic visual identity — wellness-inspired colour palette, approachable typography, and imagery direction that feels personal not corporate.
4
Mobile-First Design
Designed for mobile first — since the primary traffic source is Instagram, the mobile experience was prioritised. Desktop was an enhancement, not the base.
5
Conversion Optimisation
Placed CTAs strategically throughout the page — not just at the bottom. Every section had a natural next step for the user to take.

Learnings

What I learned

For service-based businesses, the biggest conversion lever is reducing uncertainty — showing who you are, what you do, and exactly how to get started, in that order.
Mobile-first isn't just about screen size — it's about designing for a mindset. Mobile users are browsing quickly, so every section needs to earn the scroll.
Testimonials placed early in the page — not just at the bottom — dramatically improve trust before the user even reaches the CTA.
